###  Profile Summary
I am an **Analog & Mixed-Signal IC Design Researcher** focused on high-resolution continuous-time Sigma-Delta Modulators. I specialize in designing and laying out multi-bit **90 nm CMOS** architectures with OTA offset self-calibration to mitigate deep-submicron non-idealities. 

Proficient in full-custom design from architectural modeling to post-layout (PEX) validation, emphasizing linearity and PVT robustness. I bridge circuit-level expertise with semiconductor physics and DLTS-based characterization to develop reliability-aware interfaces for sensing and edge-intelligence.

> *The analog designer's job is not to fight physics -> it is to bend the tradeoffs in your favor.*

### Technical Datasheet & Core Competencies

#### Analog & Mixed-Signal IC Design
* **Data Converters:** Continuous-time ($OTA-C$) modulators, multi-bit DACs, and Data Weighted Averaging (DWA) architectural layout.
* **Transistor-Level Blocks:** Operational Transconductance Amplifiers (OTA), dynamic comparators, bias circuits, and high-stability bandgap references (BGR).
* **Circuit Techniques:** Offset self-calibration, background calibration, and non-ideality mitigation.

#### Circuit Analysis & Verification Methodologies
* **Noise & Linearity:** Detailed thermal/flicker noise analysis, harmonic distortion, and linearity optimization.
* **Statistical Robustness:** Mismatch characterization, loop stability analysis, PVT-aware design, and Monte Carlo statistical studies.
* **Sign-Off:** Comprehensive post-layout parasitic extraction (PEX) validation and loop filter layout matching.

#### Device Physics & Advanced Technology Nodes
* **Silicon Foundations:** Full-custom 90 nm CMOS design flow and deep sub-micron process variation analysis.
* **Wide Bandgap & Traps:** Semiconductor device fundamentals, Gallium Nitride (GaN) power/RF devices, and deep-level transient spectroscopy (DLTS) trap characterization.
